## Source Scope

The app uses the supplied **CompTIA A+ Student Guide, Modules 1–10**, together with the supplied **official CompTIA A+ 220-1201 V15 Exam Objectives Document, Version 4.0**.

The **Specs & Standards** mode is restricted to facts supported by the supplied source material.

PoE wattage questions are included as supplemental standards detail because the official exam objectives identify the PoE standards but do not provide the wattage table.

## Study Modes

### 🆕 New Questions
Practice questions you have not answered before.

### 🎯 Weak Areas
Review questions from your wrong/weak question pool.

### 📖 Flashcard Review
Review questions and reveal the correct answer without scoring.

### 🔀 Random Practice
Practice a mixed selection of questions from all ten modules.

### ⭐ Starred Questions
Review questions you have marked for later practice.

### 🔌 Ports & Protocols
Practice TCP/UDP ports, protocols, and their purposes.

### 📏 Specs & Standards
Practice concrete facts such as speeds, lengths, frequencies, standards, ports, IP values, power, PoE, displays, CPU architecture, virtualization, cloud, printers, USB, Ethernet, and Wi-Fi.

## 3-Module Challenge

Select a range of three modules, such as **Modules 1–3** or **Modules 6–8**, and practice them together.

The challenge can also include an additional weak module when applicable.

## Progress

Question progress is stored locally in your browser using **LocalStorage**.

No account or server-side profile is required. Progress is therefore specific to the browser/device being used.

## PWA & Offline Support

The app is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

After the app has successfully loaded, supported browsers can cache the required files for offline use.

On supported mobile devices, the app can also be added to the home screen for an app-like experience.
